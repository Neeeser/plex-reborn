# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First working build, not yet released.

- Scene lifecycle support so Plex 8.45 runs when stamped with the iOS 27.1 SDK.
- Full-screen video on the folded and unfolded screens.
- The Duo's vertical status bar and the home indicator hide during video.
- One tap shows the player controls on the first try.
- The Duo's second panel is no longer treated as an external TV, which fixes taps and the status bar when unfolded.
- Desk posture (half open, crease across): video on the top half, controls on the bottom half.
- Plex's tabs show in the system's vertical tab bar, and all tab icons are the same size.
- A Plex Reborn page in Plex's Settings: choose your own tabs, and pick layout options.
- Plex's top bar buttons (side menu, cast, search, more) move into the vertical bar; detail pages get a back button there.
- A library's Recommended, Library and Categories tabs become a menu in the vertical bar instead of a second bar.
- The title bar is clear and hides while scrolling.
- Poster rows scroll under the vertical bar.
- The mini player is a floating glass card beside the tabs (or a full-width bar, or Plex's bar).
- In desk posture the bottom half shows the video reflected behind the controls.
- Plex's own sheets, such as the server picker, reach the screen edge.
- Live TV controls no longer overlap on the folded screen (Plex picks its own control layout again).
